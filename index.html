European Dilution Tracker — Beta

The tracker is a focused research project for investors and analysts who follow European small-cap companies where financing structure can materially affect the investment case.

It consolidates information that is often spread across annual reports, capital measures, bond terms, option programs and company announcements into a compact, structured view.
